# Evidence register — weekly program templates

Generated 2026-11-11 for the five default weeks in `templates/weekly/`. Every rule and exercise choice is tied to a source that was opened or resolved this session (through Europe PMC, publisher pages, or web search summaries); anything that could not be verified says so. Correlation-only evidence is labelled as such and is never presented as proof that training the exercise improves the outcome.

**Terms, in plain English.** *Intervention trial*: people were trained and an outcome was measured. *RCT*: a randomized controlled trial, where who trains is decided by chance. *Meta-analysis*: a statistical pooling of many trials. *Correlation only*: athletes who score higher on a test also throw harder; it does not show that training the test raises velocity. *Mechanistic*: explains how something works (muscle activity, joint motion) without a performance outcome. *Expert consensus*: a clinical or coaching review. *r*: a correlation coefficient from −1 to +1; 0.62 means the two measures share about 38% of their variation. *RPE*: rating of perceived exertion, 10 = nothing left. *1RM*: the heaviest load that can be lifted once. *ES*: effect size, a standardized measure of how big a change was (0.2 small, 0.5 medium, 0.8 large). *CNS*: central nervous system; a "high-CNS" day holds the week's most demanding work. *KEAT*: kinetic energy accumulation training (depth jumps and drop landings). *GRF*: ground reaction force. *IAP*: intra-abdominal pressure (bracing). *ER/IR*: external/internal rotation of the shoulder. *UCL*: the elbow's ulnar collateral ligament.
